Built to power what matters — reliably.

We're an Ethiopian renewable energy company specialising in integrated Battery Energy Storage Systems and solar power. Our promise is simple: design it right, build it once, support it for the long haul.

Who we are

One company, end-to-end.

Hemlon Energy was founded in Addis Ababa to close a gap in the Ethiopian energy market: too many suppliers sell hardware and disappear. We take the opposite approach — we assess your loads, design the system, source and install certified components, commission everything, and stay for after-sales support with a 24–48 hour response time nationwide.

Our long-term vision is to become one of East Africa's most trusted partners in solar and battery storage, eventually deploying utility-scale solar farms that feed the national grid.

Our vision

Clean, reliable, affordable solar independence across Ethiopia and East Africa.

From residential rooftops to utility-scale farms feeding the national grid — we see a future where dependable power is the norm, not a luxury.

Our mission

Deliver world-class energy systems with local commitment.

We combine globally certified hardware with genuine Ethiopian engineering capability and after-sales presence — so every installation keeps running, year after year.
